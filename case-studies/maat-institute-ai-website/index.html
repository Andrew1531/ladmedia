Ma’at Institute of AI Website Case Study

Explore LAD Media's website work for the Ma'at Institute of AI: responsive design, information-led structure, and a public home for AI and consulting work.

The challenge

The Ma’at Institute of AI needed a public web presence that could introduce the organization and present its AI and consulting focus in a credible, accessible format.

Work completed

  • Created a responsive organization website for the Ma’at Institute of AI.
  • Structured the site to introduce the institute and its areas of work.
  • Developed a restrained visual presentation suited to a professional AI organization.

Introducing the organization

An AI and consulting organization needs a public home that helps visitors understand who it is and what kind of work it presents. A technology label on its own does not provide that context. The Ma’at Institute of AI’s documented website challenge was to introduce the organization and its areas of focus in a professional format. The brief was about the public web presence, not an independent assessment of the institute’s expertise, research, or client results. This case study describes the website deliverables without treating the design as proof of those wider claims.

Information before decoration

LAD Media created a responsive organization website with an information-led page structure. The content structure introduces the institute and its areas of work, supported by a restrained visual presentation. An organization contact path is included among the delivered elements. Each part has a distinct purpose: the structure organizes the introduction, the visual direction provides a consistent presentation, and the contact path gives interested visitors a way to continue. These are documented design choices and deliverables. They do not imply that a formal accessibility audit, an AI application, or a consulting delivery platform formed part of the assignment.

A concrete website outcome

The result available for review is the completed organization website and its captured public-facing design. This gives the case study a concrete basis: readers can look at the delivered presentation rather than relying on a testimonial or an unverified performance number. The project evidence does not include a confirmed increase in consulting inquiries, search rankings, traffic, or revenue. Those results would require separate measurement and approval. The case study therefore records the website scope, its information-led approach, and the available evidence while leaving unmeasured business outcomes out of the account.
