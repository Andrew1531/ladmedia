VerifED Global Website Case Study

Explore LAD Media's VerifED Global website case study: a responsive site that guides people with foreign degrees towards their next career step in the U.S.

The challenge

VerifED Global needed a public website that explains U.S. career guidance for people educated abroad without overwhelming visitors who are new to the process.

Work completed

  • Created a responsive website for VerifED Global.
  • Organized career guidance for internationally educated job seekers into a clear page hierarchy.
  • Used a clean, approachable visual direction suited to visitors who are new to the process.

Making a complicated process understandable

People who earned a degree outside the United States often face several questions at once: how to present that degree to U.S. employers, whether an official credential evaluation is needed, and which licensing requirements apply to their field. Visitors need to find the question that matches their situation without reading everything first. That was the communication challenge behind VerifED Global’s public website. The documented brief concerned how the guidance is introduced and organized online. It was not an assessment of the guidance itself, of any credential evaluation, or of a visitor’s eligibility for a job or license. Keeping the website assignment separate from those matters is important when describing the work.

Building the public presentation

LAD Media created a responsive website and organized the guidance into a clear page hierarchy. The homepage states who the site is for, then asks visitors to pick the question they are facing. Each area explains what a visitor can do there, what to have ready, and where the guidance stops. Navigation covers About, How It Works, and Guides, with English and Spanish language options and a call to action to start a profile. This is the distinction between explaining a service and proving every claim about it: the site can introduce the offer and direct a visitor toward the next step, but the website design itself does not validate outcomes for any individual.

The scope of the result

The documented result is a completed website with a public address, a branded presentation, and a captured project screenshot. These materials support the claim that a website was delivered and show how its public-facing story is presented. They do not show a measured increase in profiles started, qualified inquiries, or organic traffic. No such performance outcome is claimed here. Readers can review the live site alongside the screenshot, bearing in mind that later changes to a public website may differ from the delivered view recorded in this case study.
