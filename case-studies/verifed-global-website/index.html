VerifED Global Website Case Study

Explore LAD Media's VerifED Global website case study: a responsive product website, clear page hierarchy, and a public presentation for its Web3 platform.

The challenge

VerifED Global needed a public website capable of introducing an education technology product in a Web3 context without losing clarity for visitors.

Work completed

  • Created a responsive marketing website for VerifED Global.
  • Organized the public-facing product story into a clear page hierarchy.
  • Used a technology-focused visual direction consistent with the platform’s positioning.

Making a technical product understandable

An education technology product in a Web3 setting has more than one thing to explain. Visitors need to understand the organization and the product story without having to decode the technology category first. That was the communication challenge behind VerifED Global’s public website. The documented brief concerned how the platform was introduced online. It was not an assessment of the underlying product, the accuracy of its verification process, or the status of any credentials. Keeping the website assignment separate from those product claims is important when describing the work.

Building the public presentation

LAD Media created a responsive marketing website and organized the product story into a clear page hierarchy. The technology-focused visual direction supports the platform’s positioning, while the page structure gives the public-facing information an order. Platform calls to action are part of the delivered website. This is the distinction between explaining a product and proving every claim about it: the site can introduce the offer and direct a visitor toward the next step, but the website design itself does not validate the product’s capabilities, security, or institutional acceptance.

The scope of the result

The documented result is a completed product website with a public address, a branded presentation, and a captured project screenshot. These materials support the claim that a website was delivered and show how its public-facing story was presented. They do not show a measured increase in signups, completed verifications, qualified inquiries, or organic traffic. No such performance outcome is claimed here. Readers can review the live site alongside the screenshot, bearing in mind that later changes to a public website may differ from the delivered view recorded in this case study.
