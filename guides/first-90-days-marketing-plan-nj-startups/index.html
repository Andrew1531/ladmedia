A first 90 days marketing plan for New Jersey startups

Startups & Web3 · November 25, 2026 · 6 min read · By Andrew, LAD Media, Newark, NJ

A new company cannot do everything at once, and trying usually means doing nothing well. A 90 day plan gives you three clear steps: lay the foundation, test a few channels, then put money behind what works.

Month one: foundation

  1. Clear message and website

    A one-sentence explanation of what you do, a simple site, and a way to capture email addresses or bookings.

  2. Tracking

    Analytics and goal tracking in place so every later test can be measured.

  3. Your first audience list

    Friends of the business, early customers and community members who agree to hear from you.

Month two: test a few channels

  1. Pick two or three channels

    For example search, one social platform and local or industry outreach. Do not spread across everything.

  2. Small, time-boxed tests

    Set a small budget and a fixed end date for each test, and write down what you expect.

  3. Talk to users

    Ask new customers or community members how they found you and what almost stopped them.

Month three: double down

Compare results across channels using cost per lead or cost per customer, not likes or visits. Move budget to the best performer and stop the weakest.

Turn the best content and answers from month two into your standard pages and posts.

If you are launching a Web3 project

Check each ad platform’s current crypto policy before planning paid campaigns, publish verifiable details such as policy IDs and supply, and get legal advice before describing any token sale.
