Google Business Profile checklist for New Jersey home service businesses

Home services · November 18, 2026 · 6 min read · By Andrew, LAD Media, Newark, NJ

When someone in New Jersey searches for an HVAC company or a plumber, the map results often appear before any website. Your Google Business Profile decides whether you show up there. This checklist covers what to set up, what to keep doing, and the mistakes that hold profiles back.

The six-point checklist

Work through these once. Most take a few minutes in your Google Business Profile dashboard.

  1. Choose the right primary category

    Pick the one that matches your main service, such as "HVAC contractor" or "Plumber". Add secondary categories only for services you actually offer.

  2. Set your service area, not just an address

    If you go to customers, list the New Jersey towns and counties you cover. If customers do not visit your location, hide your address and use a service area instead.

  3. Fill in every service

    Add each service with a short description in plain words, such as "furnace repair" or "water heater installation". These help Google match you to specific searches.

  4. Add real photos

    Your team, your vehicles, finished jobs. Add a few every month rather than all at once, and skip stock photos.

  5. Ask every customer for a review

    Send your review link by text or email after each job. Ask every customer, not only the happy ones, never offer anything in return, and reply to every review.

  6. Post once a week

    Share a finished job, a seasonal tip, or an offer, with a button that links to the matching page on your website.

What to do every week

A profile that stays active tends to stay visible. Fifteen minutes a week is enough:

  1. Reply to new reviews

    Thank people for positive reviews and answer negative ones calmly, with a way to follow up offline.

  2. Add photos from recent jobs

    Two or three real photos keep the profile current and show the kind of work you do.

  3. Publish one update

    A short post about a job, a seasonal reminder, or a new service, linked to your website.

  4. Check your hours

    Update hours before holidays and storms so customers do not call or drive over when you are closed.

Common mistakes

  1. Adding keywords or town names to your business name

    Use your real business name only. Extra words like "Best HVAC Newark" break Google’s guidelines and can get a profile suspended.

  2. One profile per town

    A business gets one profile. Cover more towns through your service area and website pages, not extra listings.

  3. Using a PO box or virtual office

    Google does not accept these as business addresses. If you work from home, hide the address and set a service area.

  4. Details that do not match

    Keep your business name, phone number, and website the same on your profile, your site, and directories.

Where to start

If you only do one thing this week, check your primary category and service area. They decide which searches you can appear in at all.

Then set a simple routine: ask for a review after every job, and add one update and a few photos each week. Results depend on your market and competitors, so measure calls and website visits from the profile over a few months, not days.
