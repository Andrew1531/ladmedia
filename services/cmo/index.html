Fractional CMO FAQ

What does a fractional CMO do for a Web3 company?

A fractional CMO provides senior marketing leadership on a part-time or scoped basis. That can include strategy, team direction, budget and vendor decisions, KPI frameworks, campaign prioritization, and leadership-level reporting, depending on the company’s needs.

How is fractional CMO support different from hiring a marketing agency?

An agency is often engaged to deliver specific services or campaigns. A fractional CMO focuses on the operating system around those services: deciding what matters most, connecting specialists to one plan, supporting the team, and giving leadership a strategic point of view.

When should a Web3 startup consider fractional marketing leadership?

It can be useful when founders are making disconnected channel decisions, a team has grown beyond informal coordination, or several partners need one accountable strategic lead. The right starting point depends on your goals, resources, and current marketing setup.
