A practical approach to marketing consulting

LAD Media’s marketing consulting service gives Web3 teams a structured outside perspective when the next marketing decision is important but the path is unclear. We help founders and marketing teams examine positioning, audience needs, competitive context, channels, launch plans, and the practical work required to turn a strategy into action.

Consulting can be focused on one decision or used as an ongoing advisory relationship. We tailor the depth of the work to the question in front of you, whether that means auditing an existing program, shaping a go-to-market plan, planning a campaign, preparing a team workshop, or creating a sequence of priorities for the next stage of growth. Recommendations are grounded in your product, resources, and market context rather than a generic playbook.

Who this service is for

  • Marketing teams that need an impartial audit of their current strategy and channels
  • Web3 products entering a new category, audience, market, or launch phase

How the engagement works

Frame the business question

We agree on the decision or outcome the engagement should support, gather the relevant product and market context, and define what a useful recommendation needs to address.

Study the evidence

We review existing marketing materials, audience assumptions, channel activity, competitor positioning, campaign plans, and team capacity to separate signals from assumptions.

Shape the recommendation

We translate the findings into prioritized strategic options, messaging and channel considerations, campaign ideas, or a go-to-market sequence that the team can evaluate.

Make it actionable

We document next steps, owners, dependencies, and decision points, then support the team through a workshop, planning session, or advisory cadence where appropriate.

Useful outcomes

  • A sharper view of the audience, market context, and marketing challenge
  • Prioritized recommendations that are easier for the team to evaluate and use
  • A more coherent go-to-market or campaign plan tied to available resources
  • Greater confidence about what to do next, what to defer, and why

Marketing Consulting FAQ

What can a Web3 marketing consultant help with?

Consulting can cover go-to-market strategy, competitive analysis, marketing audits, campaign planning, team workshops, and ongoing advisory support. We scope the work around a defined business question rather than forcing every project into the same process.

Do we need a complete marketing team before engaging a consultant?

No. Consulting can help a founder clarify the first marketing priorities, help an existing team make better decisions, or provide structure while responsibilities and partners are still being established.

What should we prepare for a marketing audit or strategy engagement?

Useful inputs include your product and business goals, target audiences, existing positioning, active channels, prior campaign materials, team or partner responsibilities, and any constraints that affect execution. We can identify the right starting materials during the initial scoping conversation.
