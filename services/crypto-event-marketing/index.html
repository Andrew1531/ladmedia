Crypto & Web3 Event Marketing

A crypto event marketing agency should do more than announce a date. LAD Media helps Web3 teams connect the invitation, live experience and follow-up so the right people know why to attend and what to do next.

Make each event moment part of one campaign

Whether you are planning a community meetup, product demonstration, conference activation or online session, marketing starts with the audience and a useful reason to participate. We can shape the event message, build a clear registration path and coordinate appropriate promotion across the channels that match the brief.

Event operations such as venue booking, ticketing contracts, security and on-site production should be scoped separately with the appropriate providers. We focus on marketing planning, content and coordination, and we do not claim attendance, press coverage or lead volume before results exist.

  • Web3 teams announcing a product or community milestone at an event
  • Crypto brands organizing a meetup or side event for a defined audience
  • Founders preparing an online session, panel or product demonstration
  • Teams seeking a coordinated follow-up plan after an activation

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of crypto events can you help market?

Marketing support can be scoped for meetups, conference side events, product demonstrations, virtual sessions and community gatherings. The approach depends on audience, format, event objective and the resources already in place.

Do you organize venues or run the event on site?

This page covers event marketing rather than venue booking or full event production. If those services are needed, identify them during scoping so roles and specialist partners can be agreed before promotion begins.

When should event marketing start?

Start early enough to clarify the audience, registration process, speakers or program, and approval of claims before outreach begins. The timeline varies by event type; we can plan a realistic sequence around your confirmed date.

How do you measure event marketing?

We first agree on useful signals such as qualified registrations, inquiry sources, attendance where tracked, and post-event conversations. We do not promise a fixed number of attendees or leads.
