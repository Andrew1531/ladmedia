Influencer Marketing FAQ

How do you choose influencers for a Web3 campaign?

We begin with the campaign audience and objective, then review a creator's subject matter, recent content, audience context, communication style, and practical fit for the brief. A large following by itself is not enough; the creator should be able to explain the project in a way their audience can use.

What does influencer campaign management cover?

Depending on scope, management can cover creator research, outreach, negotiations, agreements, briefing, content coordination, review, publishing schedules, disclosure reminders, and post-campaign reporting. We agree responsibilities and approval points before outreach begins.

Do influencers create their own content?

Usually, yes. Strong campaigns give creators a clear factual brief and boundaries while leaving room for their authentic format and perspective. LAD Media helps your team review content for accuracy, message alignment, and required disclosures without turning every creator post into a scripted advertisement.
