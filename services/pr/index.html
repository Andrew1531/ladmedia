PR & Media Coverage FAQ

What kind of news can a Web3 PR campaign support?

Potential angles include a product or protocol launch, a meaningful feature release, original research, a partnership with a clear user impact, company news, or an informed perspective on an industry topic. The story needs a specific point and supportable information; a generic promotion is rarely enough on its own.

Can you guarantee media coverage or a particular publication?

No. Editorial decisions belong to each journalist and publication, so responsible PR cannot guarantee coverage, placement, or a specific outlet. We can help improve the story, target relevant contacts, prepare your team, and manage the process transparently.

How does PR differ from a paid media placement?

PR outreach seeks editorial consideration and is subject to a publication's independent judgment. A paid placement is purchased media with different control, disclosure, and approval conditions. We can help clarify which communications route fits a particular objective instead of presenting paid exposure as independent coverage.
