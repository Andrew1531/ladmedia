Token Launch Strategy FAQ

What does a token launch strategy include?

It can include token utility and positioning, distribution and vesting communication, economic-model review, launch sequencing, audience education, and a content plan for the website, whitepaper, and community channels. The exact scope depends on how far the product and token design have progressed.

Can LAD Media help if our tokenomics are still being designed?

Yes. Early involvement lets us connect product goals, incentive decisions, and audience education before those choices are presented publicly. We can help structure the model and its communications, while your legal, tax, and financial advisers address matters that require their professional advice.

How do you communicate tokenomics to people who are not specialists?

We start with the user problem and token role, then explain supply, allocation, unlocks, and participation mechanics in plain language supported by diagrams or examples where useful. Technical detail remains available, but the main path should answer what the token does and why the design is relevant.
